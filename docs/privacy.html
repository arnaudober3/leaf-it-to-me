Privacy Policy

Leaf It to Me · Last updated 24 November 2026

Leaf It to Me is a game made by Arnaud Ober, an independent developer. This policy explains what happens to your data when you play it. The short version: nothing leaves your device.

Data we collect

None. The game does not collect, store, or share any personal information or usage data. In particular, it has:

  • no account or sign-in,
  • no advertising,
  • no analytics, tracking, or crash reporting,
  • no in-app purchases,
  • no third-party SDKs,
  • no access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or location.

The game works fully offline and does not connect to the internet.

Apple

The game is distributed through the App Store. Apple may collect information related to your download and use of apps, under Apple's Privacy Policy. If you choose to share app analytics with developers in your device settings, Apple may provide aggregated, anonymous statistics (such as installs or crashes) to the developer. This data does not identify you.

Children

Because the game collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Contacting support

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to answer you, and we do not share them with anyone. You can ask us to delete that correspondence at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
