Support

Leaf It to Me

Found a bug, have a question, or an idea? Write to hello@arnaudober.com. I usually reply within a few days.

When reporting a problem, it helps to include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened just before the issue.

How to play

  • Drag a leaf and release it outside the screen to get rid of it. Leaves released inside the yard drop back down.
  • A new leaf falls every few seconds, so keep sweeping.
  • When no leaf is left, a ring fills up. Keep the yard clear for one second to win.

Common questions

Do I need an internet connection?
No. The game works fully offline.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?
No.

The game isn't responding.
Close it from the app switcher and open it again. If the problem continues, update iOS and the game, then contact me.
